---
title: "Guestnet Integration"
description: "How to connect Guestnet to DialogShift and import activities and menus as knowledge for Chat AI."
---

The Guestnet integration imports activities and menus from your Guestnet account into the DialogShift knowledge base. Chat AI can use this content in its answers. Guestnet is not a booking engine and is not used for Journey Messaging.

## Prerequisites

- Your hotel uses Guestnet.
- You have your hotel's Client ID from Guestnet.

## Activate the integration

1. In the DialogShift app, open **Settings → Integrations** and select **Guestnet**.
2. Select the language of the content to import.
3. Enter your hotel's **Client ID**.
4. Activate the integration and save the settings.

The integration page also shows how many articles were read and when the content was last updated.

## Imported content

DialogShift imports the following from the activity calendar:

- title and description
- date, time, location, and duration
- categories and registration information
- direct link to the activity
- overviews for the next seven days and by category

DialogShift imports the following from the daily menu:

- courses and dishes
- descriptions and prices
- wine prices per glass or bottle
- allergen information

The content is synchronized with Guestnet automatically. If you deactivate the integration, the imported content is removed from the knowledge base.

## Chat in the Guestnet app

Importing Guestnet content and embedding the DialogShift chat in the Guestnet app are independent of each other. The chat can also be provided as a separate channel in the Guestnet app.

## Configuration

| Field | Purpose |
|:------|:--------|
| Client ID | Assigns the connection to the correct hotel in Guestnet. |
| Language | Sets the language of the imported content. |

If content is missing, check the Client ID, selected language, and whether the integration is active.
