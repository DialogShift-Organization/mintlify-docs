In the settings of the **DialogShift Email AI V2** add-in, you define how the AI phrases replies, how your emails look, and how the add-in behaves.

<video controls className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/EmailAIv2Settings_web.mp4"></video>

## Open the settings

1. Open the **DialogShift Email AI** sidebar in Outlook (via **Reply** → **Apps**).
2. Click the **gear icon** in the top right of the sidebar.

The settings menu has three tabs: **Instructions**, **Font & Signature**, and **Plugin**.

Use the **back arrow** to the left of the "Settings" heading to return to reply generation.

## "Instructions" tab

Here you control how the AI phrases replies and which rules it follows. Click **Save** at the bottom to apply your changes.

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Salutation & closing** | Fixed phrases for greeting and sign-off, e.g. "Greet every guest with 'Moin Moin!'". Enter only salutation and closing rules here, no general tone rules. |
| **Writing style** | General writing style and recurring phrases, e.g. "Use warm, casual language". |
| **Detect form of address automatically** | Enabled: The AI adopts the form of address (formal/informal) from the guest's message. Disabled: The stored default form of address is always used. |
| **Ask for missing information** | Enabled: If the guest's details are incomplete, the AI asks targeted follow-up questions instead of creating a reply with gaps. |
| **Suggest switching AI knowledge** | Checks whether the inquiry belongs to another of your properties and, if so, suggests switching the knowledge area. |
| **About [property name]** | Free text for temporary or specific additional information that is not in the knowledge base, e.g. "Expect noise from construction work on the golf course over Easter". |
| **Generate booking links** | Inserts direct booking links or booking buttons into suitable replies. |
| **Check availability** | Takes live availability into account for booking inquiries. |

## "Font & Signature" tab

Here you adapt text and buttons to the design of your property.

<Note>
Advanced formatting is available in Outlook and Mozilla Thunderbird.
</Note>

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Booking button color** | Hex color code (e.g. `#44289D`) or selection via the color picker. |
| **Font** | Selection from the dropdown, e.g. "System font". Universal fonts are supported that render reliably on all devices. |
| **Font color** | Hex color code for the text color (default: `#000000`). |
| **Signature** | Your email signature. Use the editor toolbar to format text (bold, italic, links, lists, images). |

## "Plugin" tab

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Generate reply automatically on open** | Enabled: The AI creates the draft as soon as the add-in opens, without clicking "Generate reply". |
| **Language** | Language of the add-in's user interface. |
| **Log out** | Logs the current user account out of the add-in. |

The installed version of the add-in is shown at the bottom.
