With the **DialogShift Email AI V2** add-in, you create reply drafts to guest inquiries in Outlook. The reply is based on the knowledge base and the conditions of your property.

<video controls className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/EmailAIv2Generate_web.mp4"></video>

## Requirements

- The Email AI V2 add-in is installed in Outlook. See [Email AI Plugin for Microsoft Outlook](/en/email-ai/microsoft-outlook-plugin).

## 1. Open the add-in

1. Open the incoming guest email in Outlook.
2. Click **Reply**.
3. Click **Apps** in the top menu bar and select **DialogShift Email AI**.

The DialogShift sidebar opens on the right.

## 2. Create a reply draft

Click **Generate reply** in the sidebar.

The AI analyzes the inquiry, matches it against the knowledge base, and creates a reply suggestion.

## 3. Adjust the draft (optional)

### Check and change booking details

If the AI detects travel dates or the number of guests in the inquiry, it shows them as a summary at the top of the sidebar.

1. Click the **booking icon** to open **Edit booking requests**.
2. Adjust the travel period and the number of adults and children (including ages).
3. Click **Update**. The calculation and the reply draft are recreated.

### Control tone and additional details

1. Enter an instruction in the **Additional instructions (optional)** field at the bottom of the sidebar, e.g. "Make the tone more formal" or "Mention the late check-out price".
2. Click the update arrow. The draft is rewritten accordingly.

Use this field to control the length as well, e.g. "Reply in bullet points" or "Keep the reply short".

## 4. Insert the reply and send

1. Click **Insert this reply**. The text is transferred to the reply field of your email.
2. Check key details such as availability, prices, and number of guests, and make final adjustments.
3. Click **Send** in Outlook.
