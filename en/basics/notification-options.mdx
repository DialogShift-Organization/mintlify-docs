DialogShift offers various notification options that can be configured both in the user profile and across hotels.

## User Profile Settings

For your own user, notifications can be activated under [Organization >> Notifications](https://app.dialogshift.com/notifications).

### 1. Email Notifications

Activation for the email address you signed in with.

<Note>
  Additional email addresses can only be added across hotels (see below).
</Note>

### 2. Smartphone / Mobile Device

Push notifications via the DialogShift Mobile App.

### 3. Browser / Desktop

<Note>
  Prerequisite: Live Chat App must be open in a browser tab
</Note>

The audio signal is played by the Live Chat App itself and works in all supported browsers.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Chrome ⭐">
    **Windows/Mac** (Recommended)

    - ✅ Notifications are displayed
    - ✅ Optional persistent notifications
    - ✅ Audio signal
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Edge">
    **Windows/Mac**

    - ✅ Notifications are displayed
    - ✅ Optional persistent notifications
    - ✅ Audio signal
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Firefox">
    **Windows/Mac**

    - ✅ Notifications (permission must be granted via click)
    - ✅ Persistent notifications on Windows only
    - ✅ Audio signal
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Safari">
    **Mac**

    - ✅ Notifications (permission must be granted via click)
    - ❌ No persistent notifications (see note on macOS)
    - ✅ Audio signal
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  By default, macOS displays notifications as "Banners" that disappear automatically after a few seconds. To keep notifications visible, set your browser's notification style to "Alerts" in the macOS System Settings under "Notifications".
</Note>
